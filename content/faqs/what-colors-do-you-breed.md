We specialize in British Shorthair, British Longhair, Scottish Fold, and Scottish Straight cats in a beautiful variety of colors and patterns. Our breeding program includes: blue, lilac, cream, blue cream, blue cream tabby, blue shaded, chocolate golden shaded, silver tabby, and other variations. We also work with both shorthair and longhair coat varieties. Each color and pattern combination has its own unique charm. Check our breeding cats and available kittens pages to see our current colors and upcoming litters.
