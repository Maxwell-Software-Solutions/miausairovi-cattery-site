Our British Shorthair, British Longhair, Scottish Fold, and Scottish Straight kittens are priced between £800-£1,500 depending on breed, color, pedigree quality, and coat pattern. The price includes first and second vaccinations (at 8 and 12 weeks), health check, microchip, GCCF registration, 5-generation pedigree certificate, breeder slip, and a kitten starter pack with food and toys.
