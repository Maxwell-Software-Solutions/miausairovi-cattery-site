question: What is included when I purchase a kitten?
category: adoption

Your kitten comes with: GCCF registration certificate, 5-generation pedigree certificate, breeder slip, vaccination record (first vaccination at 8 weeks, second at 12 weeks), microchip, health check certificate, food sample, toy with familiar scent, care guide, and lifetime breeder support. We're always here to answer questions!
